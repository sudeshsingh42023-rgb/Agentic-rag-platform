# GLOBAL PROCUREMENT AND VENDOR ENGAGEMENT POLICY
Policy reference GPP-2026-01. Supersedes GPP-2023-04.

## 1. APPROVAL THRESHOLDS
1.1 Purchases up to INR 2,00,000 may be approved by the requesting department head.
1.2 Purchases above INR 2,00,000 and up to INR 25,00,000 require approval from the category procurement manager.
1.3 Purchases above INR 25,00,000 require approval from the procurement council and a documented competitive bid.
1.4 Any single-source award above INR 10,00,000 requires a written single-source justification approved by the chief procurement officer.

## 2. COMPETITIVE BIDDING
2.1 A minimum of three qualified bids is required for all purchases above INR 25,00,000.
2.2 The bid window shall remain open for no fewer than 14 calendar days.
2.3 Bid evaluation shall apply a weighted scorecard: 40% technical fit, 30% commercial terms, 20% delivery capability and 10% sustainability performance.

## 3. VENDOR ONBOARDING
3.1 All vendors shall complete due diligence covering financial stability, sanctions screening and information security posture before the first purchase order is raised.
3.2 Vendors processing RESTRICTED data shall additionally complete a security assessment aligned to standard ERS-014 and shall be reassessed annually.
3.3 Vendor master data changes, including bank account changes, require verification through an independently sourced contact.

## 4. PAYMENT TERMS
4.1 The standard payment term is net 45 days from receipt of a valid invoice.
4.2 Early payment discounts may be accepted where the annualised benefit exceeds 8%.
4.3 Advance payments are prohibited except where approved by the chief financial officer and secured by a bank guarantee.

## 5. CONFLICTS OF INTEREST
5.1 Employees shall declare any personal or familial interest in a vendor before participating in a sourcing decision.
5.2 Gifts from vendors exceeding INR 5,000 in value shall be declined or surrendered to the compliance office.

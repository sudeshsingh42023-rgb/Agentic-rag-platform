# HORIZON HEALTH SHIELD POLICY - TERMS AND CONDITIONS
Document version 3.2. Effective 01 April 2026.

## 1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Policy Year" means a period of twelve consecutive months from the commencement date shown in the schedule.
1.2 "Pre-existing Disease" means any condition, ailment or injury diagnosed by a physician within 48 months prior to the commencement of the first policy issued by the insurer.
1.3 "Network Hospital" means a hospital that has entered into an agreement with the insurer for cashless treatment.

## 2. WAITING PERIODS
2.1 An initial waiting period of 30 days applies from the commencement date, during which no claim is payable except for accidental injury.
2.2 Pre-existing diseases are covered after a continuous waiting period of 36 months.
2.3 Specified illnesses listed in Annexure B, including cataract, hernia and joint replacement, are covered after a waiting period of 24 months.
2.4 Maternity benefit is available after a continuous waiting period of 24 months and is limited to two deliveries during the lifetime of the policy.

## 3. CLAIM INTIMATION AND SUBMISSION
3.1 For planned hospitalisation the insured must notify the insurer at least 48 hours prior to admission.
3.2 For emergency hospitalisation intimation must be given within 24 hours of admission.
3.3 Complete claim documents must reach the insurer within 15 days of discharge. Claims submitted after 15 days may be considered if the delay is satisfactorily explained, at the sole discretion of the insurer.
3.4 The insurer shall settle or reject a claim within 30 days of receipt of the last necessary document. Where an investigation is required, settlement shall be completed within 45 days.

## 4. SUB-LIMITS AND CO-PAYMENT
4.1 Room rent is capped at 1% of the sum insured per day for normal rooms and 2% for intensive care units.
4.2 A co-payment of 20% applies to all claims where the insured person is above 60 years of age at the time of the claim.
4.3 Ambulance charges are reimbursed up to INR 5,000 per hospitalisation.
4.4 Cataract surgery is capped at INR 40,000 per eye per policy year.

## 5. EXCLUSIONS
5.1 Cosmetic or plastic surgery, unless necessitated by accident, burns or cancer.
5.2 Treatment arising from participation in hazardous sports including scuba diving, para-gliding and motor racing.
5.3 Expenses related to any admission primarily for evaluation or diagnostic purposes.
5.4 Dental treatment, unless arising from an accident and requiring hospitalisation.

## 6. RENEWAL AND PORTABILITY
6.1 The policy is renewable for life provided renewal premium is received before the expiry date.
6.2 A grace period of 30 days is allowed for renewal. Coverage is not available for the period of the break.
6.3 The insured may port the policy to another insurer by applying at least 45 days but not more than 60 days before the renewal date.
6.4 A cumulative bonus of 10% of the sum insured is granted for each claim-free year, up to a maximum of 50%.

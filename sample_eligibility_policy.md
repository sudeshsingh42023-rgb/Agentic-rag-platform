## 1. Eligibility Criteria

### 1.1 Academic credentials
Candidates must be graduating in 2027 with a B.E, B.Tech, M.E or M.Tech degree.
A consistent academic record with a minimum of 60% (or equivalent CGPA) in
Class X, Class XII, Diploma, UG and PG is required. CGPA-to-percentage
conversion follows the candidate's university norms.

### 1.2 Backlog policy
Students with standing arrears at the time of the interview are not eligible
to apply. A cleared backlog with an updated marksheet is acceptable.

### 1.3 Nationality and location
The program is open to Indian nationals and OCIs currently residing in India.
Candidates must be flexible to work from any office location, in a given shift
timing, on a given technology stack, as assigned.

## 2. Hiring Process

### 2.1 Registration and screening
Candidates register with a resume (maximum two pages) carrying a recent,
high-resolution passport-size photograph on a light background, with both
ears visible.

### 2.2 Assessment rounds
Round 1 is an online aptitude and communication assessment. Round 2 is an
in-person technical assessment. Only candidates who clear Round 2 proceed to
Round 3, an in-person technical interview.

### 2.3 Role fitment
Every shortlisted candidate is first evaluated for the senior associate
level. If not selected at that level, fitment for the associate level is
assessed in the same interview, at the organization's sole discretion.

## 3. Compensation

Compensation depends on the role assigned after interview. The associate
level and senior associate level carry different CTC bands; the exact figure
is confirmed in the Letter of Intent issued after selection.

## 4. Onboarding Requirements

A PAN card is mandatory for onboarding; the name on it must exactly match the
name and date of birth on the candidate's Class X marksheet, or background
verification will fail. A Passport or Voter ID is required to verify
nationality. An Aadhaar card is mandatory for onboarding.
